### 8.1 The predeclared evaluation (version 1)

[ew] marks v1 "frozen 2026-10-17 before any MESH grid value was decoded by this workstream" and says a change needs a new version and a stated reason (status). Four things qualify that: the W1 Benchland value was on file when the spatial rule was written (spatial_match); the review then wrote the report-coordinate centre into the frozen text with no new version or reason (review.corrections; f61); the strata cite DECISIONS D24 for the radar sites, which the correction places in the predeclaration, line 81; and the text cites D17's 5 mile (about 8 km) case, which the radius list [5, 10, 25 km] omits. The held-out day list is not final ("name one reference first", splits).

- **Report hit or miss.** A report at or above a size class is a hit if a MESH cell at or above that class lies within R km on the same SPC day. R = 15 km primary; 5, 10 and 25 km sensitivity (spatial_match; f8, f61). Reported as hits, misses and POD = hits / (hits + misses), with Wilson 95% and day-block bootstrap intervals.
- **Object false alarm.** A connected set of cells at or above a class with no report at or above it within R km. Reported per object, as an upper bound because reports are incomplete.
- **Size error and conditional reliability.** For hits, median and mean absolute error in inches and mm. For grid points, the share with a report at or above the class, by MESH bin: a lower bound on the chance of hail. Calibration and Brier score are not reported, because Montana has no complete reference (metrics).
- **Counts and exclusions.** Always n, hits, misses, objects and days; no rate for a class with fewer than 30 reports in the held-out window. A day with a documented gap in a reference is excluded for that reference, never read as no hail (metrics; exclusions).
- **IEM LSR (primary, all rows and M-only).** VALID in UTC, MAG in inches, QUALIFIER E, M or U. 2025 Montana: 307 rows; 225 at 0.75 in or more; 192 at 1.00 in or more (f33). E, M and U are not defined in any IEM or NWS document read (f35, false). 27 rows say "estimated" in REMARK (f36).
- **SPC daily raw hail (secondary).** 189 Montana rows in 2025 (f57). Daily files hold 1.00 in and up only (f56) and largely repeat the LSR reports, so they are not independent.
- **SPC annual and Storm Events (counts; completeness).** Annual 190 and Storm Events 190, with 61 at 1.75 in or more (f57). The SPC page says 0.75 in hail continues in the annual files ([pr] review.reviewer_checks), so the 0.75 class has a source the file does not name.
- **SWDI nx3hail (radar-algorithm cross-check, not ground truth; not counted here).** Gaps on 2025-01-12 to 19 ([sw] review.corrections) and after 03:29 UTC on 2025-06-15 in the Montana box (ew f40). MAXSIZE units unverified (ew f41, false).

Not references: MESH itself, NWS warnings, SPC outlooks, model fields and inspection records, which do not yet exist (reference_observations).

Splits: development 2020-10-14 to 2024-12-31 is a pipeline check only; held-out is 2025 except 2025-06-15 (the known example); 2026 on is reserved (splits). The named hail days come from the globe list: six under the SPC-filtered rule and twelve with the IEM rule (ew f68). That list counted filtered reports (10, 5 and 11; raw 18, 8 and 14; f7). Sensitivity runs: window plus or minus 6 h, M-only LSR, raw versus filtered SPC, and without social-media rows (23 of 123 filtered 2025 rows; f67).

- **Centre choice flips the class.** On the 2025-06-16 12:00 file, the MESH maximum within 15 km of the SPC point (47.08 N, 110.02 W) is 51.3 mm (2.02 in): a hit on the 1.75 in report (44.45 mm). Within 15 km of the brief's point (47.0 N, 109.9 W, 12.72 km away) the MESH maximum is 35.7 mm (1.41 in): a miss. That point is equidistant from four grid nodes, so no nearest value exists (ew f8, f61; [mrms] f52). Among the reviewed files, only ew f61 computes the SPC-point value.
- **Held-out lists and counts depend on the reference.** On 2025-06-15 the Montana hail rows are 33 raw and 16 filtered (ew f5); the SPC filter keeps the largest of the rows within 10 miles and 15 minutes (f55). The 2025 Montana hail rows are IEM 307, SPC raw 189, SPC filtered 123 and Storm Events 190 (f57).

### 8.2 Wording rules

- Each per-location sentence names its evidence kind (radar estimate, point report, station observation, modeled wind, official alert, forecast or inspection), source, record or cell, time (UTC and local zone), resolution and original unit. Kinds stay apart. A count reads "addresses inside the estimated footprint", never "damaged roofs" or "customers". Missing input reads "Data unavailable" ([ew] wording.principles). Templates are in section 4.8.
- The three required lines, verbatim ([ew] wording.required_lines): "No report is not "no hail", and no radar estimate is not "no hail". Missing data is shown as Data unavailable, not as an all-clear." / "This shows weather near this location. It does not show that a roof was damaged, and it is not an insurance determination." / "Weather exposure does not confirm roof damage. This is an internal summary, not certified weather evidence." (DECISIONS D17)
- Every rendered sentence is string-checked against the forbidden list (for example "confirmed damage", "insurance eligible", "customers", "best", "approved"), and each page is checked in a browser ([ew] wording.check; AGENTS rule 8).
- Fix before use: (a) the radar_estimate template prints inches and mm, but MESH units are unverified ([mrms] f22; ew f9), so no MESH size prints until the H0 units gate in section 9 closes; (b) E and M print as "estimated" and "measured", but no document defines them, so print the letter as coded (ew f35); (c) AGENTS.md says SPC hail is in hundredths and "UNK wind stays unknown"; that holds for daily files only, because yearly files are decimal inches and 153 daily UNK wind rows carry numeric yearly speeds ([pr] f13, false; review.corrections); (d) the alert template reads "Official NWS", and the NWS disclaimer protects the NWS name and visual identifier without permission (ew f23): counsel to confirm the wording.
