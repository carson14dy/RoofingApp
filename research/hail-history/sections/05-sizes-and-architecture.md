## 5. Sizes and architecture

Sizes are decimal (1 GB = 10^9 bytes). The 12:00 UTC window is 2021-10-17 to 2026-10-16: 1,826 day slots, 1,825 files. "Estimate" means scaled from a 115-day sample (summer, June to August, 81 of 459 days; shoulder, April, May, September and October, 24 of 610; winter, November to March, 10 of 756). Its 95% intervals cover sampling error only, not unit or code error. Inch values assume the file values are millimetres, which no document read confirms ([mrms-mesh-archive.json](validation-evidence/mrms-mesh-archive.json), *limits*: units and codes unverified).

### Recommendation

**Build architecture A first.** It needs no new host, it fits the rule that the map loads only site files, its data is about 40 MB against a 1 GB limit, and it answers the five-year question for every address in the index that section 3 defines. A browser lookup reads one tile: median 6,592 B, largest 310,433 B. The 100 GB soft bandwidth limit would allow about 15.2 million median-tile lookups a month (computed in the evidence; not a traffic forecast) ([hosting-and-architecture.json](validation-evidence/hosting-and-architecture.json): *architectures*, A, `per_lookup`). B waits for the owner's decision on an off-site host; it adds the raw archive for re-derivation. C waits for a decision to run a service, because it changes the static-site rule. These are design reasons; no provider is ranked.

### Gaps the evidence does not settle

- **Shards between daily runs (A).** Not in the evidence. Committing shards to main each day would grow the history against the size guidance (growth not measured), and no public artifact quota was found. A rebuild from the 0.90 GB series would need its own job: about 12 to 14 minutes by the two cited estimates (computed here), not measured on runners.
- **Units and codes.** MESH units are unverified (mm assumed), and the meanings of -1 and -3 are open; the counts treat both as no event. Parquet, vector tiles and PMTiles are not measured.
- **Schedule.** Four observed runs in one P0 sample. The evidence says a schedule needs several days of observations, and a daily job needs a retry and a manual trigger ([hosting-and-architecture.json](validation-evidence/hosting-and-architecture.json): *limits*).
- **Two-minute archive.** Whether it is needed at all is an owner question: about 69 GB, more than one runner's 14 GB disk, against 0.90 GB for the 12:00 series.
- **Decision number.** The wave-1 brief calls the off-site host decision "D32 pending", but the globe plan's D32 is the default-off storm-layers flag (`research/globe/README.md` line 542). The number needs confirming before section 9 cites it.
- **Largest tile.** It holds 33,855 addresses; whether tiles need splitting is open.
