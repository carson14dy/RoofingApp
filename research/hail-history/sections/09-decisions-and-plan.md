## 9. Decisions and plan

This section sets out the seven owner decisions (OW-H1 to OW-H7) and the staged plan H0 to H5, in the layout of the globe plan's decision table and gate table ([globe README §2 and §6](../globe/README.md)). Nothing here is built or published. No default applies. Until the owner answers OW-H2 and OW-H7, no address-level output ships; until the H0 units gate closes, no MESH size is shown.

Citations name the file and the fact: `file.json` fN is `facts[N]` in validation-evidence/file.json, and `verify.two_minute_check` is a key path. A fact that its refuter flagged (`verified: false`, or corrected in `review`) is said so in the cell. [h1-mesh-store.json](validation-evidence/h1-mesh-store.json) is a prototype record with no refuter pass, so its numbers are researcher measurements.
