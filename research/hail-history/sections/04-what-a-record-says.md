## 4. What a record says
This sets what one address record holds and the sentences it may print. It makes no hail or damage finding, and nothing per address is published until the owner and counsel decide (section 08). Citations: mrms = [mrms-mesh-archive.json](validation-evidence/mrms-mesh-archive.json), pr = [point-reports-archives.json](validation-evidence/point-reports-archives.json), swdi = [swdi.json](validation-evidence/swdi.json), warn = [warnings-archive.json](validation-evidence/warnings-archive.json), wind = [wind-history.json](validation-evidence/wind-history.json), ew = [evaluation-and-wording.json](validation-evidence/evaluation-and-wording.json). "f10" is fact 10 in that file's facts list (counted from 1); "pr R6" is that file's own fact id; "ew pre_checks" is a key in that file; "AGENTS.md:77" is a repo line.

### 4.1 The five-year window

| Rule | Decision | Why and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Length and cut | 1,826 SPC report days, each 12:00 UTC to 11:59 UTC the next day, ending at 12:00 UTC on the build date (OW-H1, section 09) | Five calendar years with the 2024 leap day: a fixed length, so builds compare, and the window moves with each build. The SPC day ([ew f70]) and the MRMS daily file stamped 12:00 UTC ([mrms f10]) share this cut |
| Research snapshot | Report days 2021-10-17 to 2026-10-16; MRMS files stamped 2021-10-18 to 2026-10-17 | W1 counted the 12:00 folders one day earlier, 2021-10-17 to 2026-10-16 ([mrms f10]). Its 2026 count stops at 10-16 with 289 files ([mrms f11]), so the 2026-10-17 file needs a check at H0 |
| Floor and build time | No data before 2021-10-17 (the 2020-10-14 day is incomplete); start the build after 13:30 UTC (a design choice) | The 2020-10-14 bucket day holds 112 frames against about 720 ([mrms f9], [mrms f56]); the IEM has no 12:00 files from 2021-10-17 to 2022-06-14 ([mrms f39]). The 12:00 file was last modified 496 to 3,623 seconds after its stamp ([ew f131]) |
| Other counts | Earlier counts in this research use calendar dates, for example SWDI from 00:00 UTC ([swdi f16]) | They sit 12 hours off this cut at each end and must be restated before a build |

### 4.2 What each block holds

| Block | Evidence and fields kept on every row | Match rule |
|---|---|---|
| Radar estimate | MRMS MESH_Max_1440min, bucket copy: file name, grid node, raw value, code, units status, centre, radius, distance | Node nearest the address; largest value within 15 km as a search area |
| Point reports | NCEI Storm Events, SPC daily and yearly, IEM LSR: source, source ID (EVENT_ID, om, LSR row), UTC and local time with zone, size or speed as recorded, unit, qualifier, distance, time gap | Within 15 km and 60 minutes, one-to-one; unmatched LSR rows stay preliminary (4.4) |
| Hail signatures | SWDI nx3hail: ZTIME (UTC), WSR_ID, CELL_ID, PROB, SEVPROB, MAXSIZE (inches), distance, radar distance | Within 15 km, same report day (4.5) |
| Official warnings | IEM SBW (SV, TO): PROD_ID, WFO, ETN, status, POLY_BEG and POLY_END (UTC), hail, wind, tornado and damage tags, match basis | Polygon containing the address at the time (4.6) |
| Wind and survey | IEM station gusts, HRRR analysis gust, NCEI High and Strong Wind, NWS DAT points: station or cell, value and unit as published, UTC time, distance | Nearest stations; High and Strong Wind by county or zone only; DAT not shown per address, no template yet (4.7) |

Forecast and inspection keep separate blocks and templates (4.8); no inspection records exist yet ([ew evaluation.not_reference]). No owner name or mailing field enters any record ([ew privacy]).

### 4.3 Radar estimate
- Cell and ties: the grid is 0.01 degree, about 1.11 km north to south and 0.73 km east to west at 49 N ([ew f79]). The test point 47.0 N, 109.9 W is 0.673 km from four nodes, so ties need round coordinates ([mrms review.corrections]).
- Units: a NOAA training page says base units are mm and AWIPS shows inches ([ew f78]); the GRIB units key reads "unknown" in the decodes ([mrms f22]). Until the units gate in section 09 closes, print the raw value with "units not verified" and no size words. Inches are mm divided by 25.4 ([ew f121]).
- Codes: -1 and -3 occur in the decodes ([mrms f26]); their meaning is unverified because the NSSL table did not load ([mrms f27], [mrms f46]). Count them, exclude them from maxima, and never read them as zero or as no hail ([ew pre_checks]). On 2025-06-16 the 43.0 to 50.5 N, 118.5 to 101.0 W test box (not Montana) had -1 on 81.86 percent of points and -3 on none ([mrms f19]); the full grid had -3 on 33.36 percent ([ew f129]). That split is a hypothesis about coverage, not a finding.
- Window: the 12:00 file equalled the maximum of 720 two-minute frames on 2025-06-15/16 ([ew f128]) but not on 2025-06-26, where 211,686 of 24,500,000 points differ from the bucket copy ([mrms f57], [mrms f15]). The record treats the file as the daily maximum product and claims no frame match for any day; the synthesis brief's claim that the 12:00 UTC file is the trailing 24 h maximum is not a general rule. The IEM copy differs on four 12:00 days ([mrms f40], [mrms f60]). A missing file, such as 2022-06-09, reads "Data unavailable", never zero ([mrms f10]).
- Limits and centre: 457.4 (2025-06-26) and 199.8 (2026-01-15, outside the US) are unexplained, and no plausibility limit is set ([mrms f28], [mrms f61]); hold values above the H0 limit for review. The 15 km maximum depends on the centre: 51.3 and 35.7 file units at points 12.72 km apart ([ew f130]). Show each value with its centre, radius and distance; 15 km is the primary radius, with 5, 10 and 25 km as sensitivity cases ([ew evaluation.spatial_match]).

### 4.4 Point reports
- Units and time: LSR hail is read as inches, which the IEM does not state ([pr f22]); SPC daily sizes are hundredths of an inch and speeds mph ([pr f5], [pr R2]); SPC yearly sizes are decimal inches, speeds knots, and times CST, UTC minus 6 hours ([pr f5], [pr f6], [pr urls_checked]); NCEI sizes are inches and speeds knots, with local MST-7 times whose daylight use the PDF does not state ([pr f30], [ew f96]). LSR and SPC daily times are UTC ([pr R2], [pr R15]).
- Matching and qualifier: 15 km, 60 minutes, one-to-one, no chaining, since chaining merged 44 LSR hail rows of 2025-06-15 into 19 clusters ([pr f36], [pr f41]). E and M agree with NCEI EG and MG in 1,037 of 1,055 pairs ([pr f19]); print E, M and U as "provisional" or "as coded" ([ew qualifier_labels]). REMARK "estimated" is mostly about the time ([pr f26]), and MAG "None" is missing data ([pr f21]).
- Small hail: SPC yearly has Montana hail under 1.00 in for 2020 to 2022 and one 0.88 in row in 2025 ([pr R6]); the 2025 raw daily files, all states, start at 1.00 in ([ew f125]); LSR has 24 to 45 Montana reports a year in 2021 to 2025 at 0.75 to 0.99 in, with no SPC or NCEI row for 2023 or 2024 ([pr R7]). An empty SPC row is not evidence of no small hail, and the synthesis brief's "SPC omits hail under 1.00 in" is not a rule.
- Time and lag: NCEI local time plus 7 hours matches LSR to the minute for 1,713 of 1,921 pairs, 89.2 percent ([pr R15]); against SPC reports, July and August matches run 60 minutes later for 41 of 73 ([ew f127]), so use a 60-minute tolerance ([ew wording.principles]). The NCEI 2026 file ends 2026-06-30 ([pr f34]); each row shows its source's last date and pull time.

### 4.5 Radar hail signatures
- Size lines use nx3hail, defined as "Filtered Hail Signatures (Max Size > 0 and Probability = 100%)" ([swdi f2]). nx3hail_all adds rows with -999 in PROB, SEVPROB and MAXSIZE whose meaning is undocumented ([swdi f13]); 99.16 percent of Montana -999 rows lie beyond 124 nmi of their radar, an inference ([swdi f37]). Use nx3hail_all only for counts, with the -999 rows counted apart. MAXSIZE is inches in steps of 0.25 ([swdi f9], [swdi f10]), with 1 to 4 km positional error ([swdi f22]); the nearest radars to the test point are KTFX at 123.2 km and KBLX at 161.4 km ([swdi f21], a secondary list).
- Counts are detections, not events: 42 nx3hail detections lay within 15 km of the test point in the 24 hours to 2025-06-15 12:00 UTC ([swdi f24]; that fact's flag covers its MRMS count only). Show counts and the largest rows. Archive gaps (2025-06-15 12:00 to 24:00 UTC, 2025-01-12 to 19, 2026-10-13) mean no data, not no hail ([swdi f4], [swdi f6], [swdi f36], [swdi terms]). Remove exact duplicates ([swdi f18]) and query in 744-hour windows ([swdi f7]).

### 4.6 Official warnings
- Covered: an SV or TO polygon contains the point with POLY_BEG at or before the time, POLY_END after it, and status not EXP ([warn f32]); times are UTC ([warn f7]). Tags state the product's stated hazard, not a measurement, and no field says radar or observed ([warn f23]). The archive ends with SV issued 2026-09-14 and TO 2025-07-31 ([warn f43]) and is a secondary copy checked by sampling ([warn limits]).
- Tags: IEM drops the "<" that the official text uses on 13 of 61 hail products, so an IEM hail value is an upper bound there ([warn f21], [warn limits]). A 0.00 tag is a stated zero ([warn f40]). HAILTAG is on every SV event from 2018 but on 84 of 543 in 2010 ([warn f16]).
- VTEC versus polygon (open): at 23:12 UTC on 2025-06-15 four TFX warnings were in VTEC effect while no polygon covered the test point ([warn f38]); at 20:20 UTC a Benchland report lay outside every polygon while ETN 43 was in effect ([warn f44]). Options: polygon only, as tested, or adding county or zone status under AGENTS.md:77-79, which this archive cannot test because it has no county-only SV or TO events ([warn limits]). Show both, labelled, until decided.
- EXP rule (open): 103 SV and 1 TO EXP rows end before they begin ([warn f6]), and skipping EXP leaves 21:51 to 22:00 UTC uncovered at the test point ([warn open_questions]). The end-of-event rule is an engineering choice, not checked against VTEC wording ([warn limits]).

### 4.7 Wind
- Reports: LSR codes G (thunderstorm gust), N (other gust), A (high sustained), D and O (damage) ([wind f14] to [wind f17]). The LSR wind unit reads as mph by remark tests (1,115 of 1,333 remarks match, [wind f54]), though the IEM does not state it. NCEI thunderstorm wind is in knots, MG or EG ([wind f5]). NCEI High and Strong Wind have no coordinates, so they show by county or zone only ([pr R19]).
- Station and model: IEM MT_ASOS has 60 stations, 42 online ([wind f28]). The source of its daily maximum is undocumented, and 29,558 of 29,568 non-whole-knot values match whole mph ([wind f52]); hold values of 130 kt and up for QC ([wind f26]). Do not print "Measured" until the source is documented. HRRR GUST is a later, model-only addition at about 3 km ([wind programs]). NWS DAT points for Montana, 2021 to 2026, are 20 points, 1 line and no polygon ([wind f55]); they are survey points, not roof inspections ([wind limits]).

### 4.8 Exact sentence templates
Copied from [ew wording](validation-evidence/evaluation-and-wording.json): templates, required_lines, qualifier_labels and forbidden_terms. Check every rendered sentence against the forbidden terms and the required lines, then browse each page (AGENTS.md:85).

- **radar_estimate**: `Radar estimate (NOAA MRMS MESH): up to {size_in} in ({size_mm} mm) (the largest cell within {radius_km} km of this location, a search area), {window_start_utc} to {window_end_utc} UTC (SPC day {day}). Estimated maximum hail size from radar, not a measurement at this address. Source: MRMS MESH_Max_1440min, 0.01 degree grid, file {file_name}.`
- **radar_no_value**: `No radar hail estimate here: the MESH cells within {radius_km} km carry code {code} (meaning not verified). This is not an all-clear.`
- **point_report**: `Reported hail: {size_in} in at {place}, {report_utc} UTC ({report_local}, {zone}), {distance_km} km away. Source: {source_name}; size recorded as {qualifier_label}. One report at one place, not a measurement at this address.`
- **station_observation**: `Measured wind gust: {gust} {unit} at {station_id} ({station_name}), {time_utc} UTC, {distance_km} km away. Station observation, not at this address.`
- **modeled_wind**: `Modeled gust: up to {gust} {unit} in the model grid cell nearest this location (about {resolution_km} km), valid {time_utc} UTC. Model analysis, not an observation.`
- **official_alert**: `Official NWS {phenomenon} {significance} for {area}, valid {start_local} to {end_local} ({zone}). Matched {match_basis}. Hail tag: {hail_tag}. Wind tag: {wind_tag}. Text from the National Weather Service; this site does not write alert text.`
- **forecast**: `Forecast ({source}, issued {issued_local}): {summary} for {valid_local}. Forecast, not observed weather.`
- **inspection**: `Inspection record ({inspection_date}, recorded by {role}): {result_as_recorded}. Kept separate from the weather evidence above.`
- **data_unavailable**: `Data unavailable: {feed_name} did not load for this location and time window ({reason}). This is not an all-clear.`
- **no_report**: `No hail report within {radius_km} km in {sources} for {window}. No report is not "no hail". Reports are scarce in rural areas.`
- **address_count**: `{count} saved properties are inside the estimated footprint for {event_name} ({class_label} hail or larger, radar estimate). This counts properties in an estimated area. It does not count damaged roofs.`
- **not_all_clear (required)**: `No report is not "no hail", and no radar estimate is not "no hail". Missing data is shown as Data unavailable, not as an all-clear.`
- **not_damage (required)**: `This shows weather near this location. It does not show that a roof was damaged, and it is not an insurance determination.`
- **export_line (every export)**: `Weather exposure does not confirm roof damage. This is an internal summary, not certified weather evidence. (DECISIONS D17)`
- **qualifier_labels**: E `estimated (provisional until the code is verified)`; M `measured (provisional until the code is verified)`; U `unknown (as coded)`.
- **forbidden_terms (never printed)**: `confirmed damage`, `damaged roof`, `damaged roofs`, `damage probability`, `chance of damage`, `insurance eligible`, `eligible for a claim`, `customers`, `hail hit your roof`, `hail on the roof`, `best`, `guaranteed`, `approved`, `safe to work`, `all clear, except inside the required not-an-all-clear line`.
