# Evidence contract for a defensible reading

This is a proposed deterministic input/claim contract, not a weather prediction algorithm or evidence of accuracy. A valid record can still contain an erroneous observation, algorithm estimate or interpretation. Use this alongside the hazard procedures and evaluation protocol. A checker should determine whether a specific statement is eligible for carefully scoped wording, needs review or is blocked by missing/incompatible evidence; never return `accurate`, `safe` or `news_certified`.

## How, what, where, when and why

- **How:** Identify the instrument, observing/processing method, provider, upstream lineage, version and quality. Separate a measured quantity, human report, algorithm estimate, forecast, official alert and simulation.
- **What:** Preserve the named variable, value kind, units and threshold/probability definitions. “Wind,” “rain” and “hail” are insufficient variable names for calculation.
- **Where:** Preserve source footprint, coordinates, coordinate reference system, resolution, sampling height and geographic domain. State separately how that evidence relates to the requested location. A nearby report or covering grid cell is not a roof measurement.
- **When:** Preserve observation/valid interval, issuance/publication, receipt, revision and decision cutoff. Report dates and timezone offsets. A recent download does not make old observations current; a forecast can validly describe a future interval.
- **Why:** Link each derived number and claim to evidence IDs, a documented method and its applicability. Distinguish the evidence-supported interpretation from plausible alternatives and remaining gaps. Agreement from shared upstream data is not independent corroboration.

## Common evidence record

Use explicit `null` for unavailable fields and machine-readable reasons; do not invent metadata. This conceptual schema can be a JSON object. Fields are conditionally required by a registered product specification rather than universally required for every source.

| Field group | Minimum contents and interpretation |
|---|---|
| Identity | `evidence_id`, `hazard`, `variable`, `evidence_kind` (`measurement`, `report`, `estimate`, `forecast`, `official_alert`, `simulation`), provider, product, product version or explicit unknown, source URL/record ID, revision and upstream lineage IDs |
| Value | `value_kind` (`instantaneous`, `rate`, `accumulation`, `interval_maximum`, `mean`, `gust`, `probability`, `category`, `event_report`, `status`), `value`, native and normalized units, conversion method; retain bounds/censoring such as “at least” or “trace” instead of inventing an exact value |
| Probability/category | For a probability: event definition, threshold and its units, interval, spatial/neighborhood definition, unconditional/conditional meaning and calibration provenance if known. For a category: published definition/version. A class label is not a numerical probability |
| Time | `valid_start`, `valid_end`, `observed_at` when applicable, `issued_at`, `published_at` if known, `received_at`, decision/as-of cutoff, explicit timezone/UTC; interval endpoint convention and product time semantics |
| Space | Source geometry/reference, CRS, grid resolution or report location precision, supported domain/version, site and elevation/range/height reference where relevant, and coverage mask/status for the requested place/time |
| Quality | Native flags, normalized `usable`/`suspect`/`unusable`/`unknown` status with reasons, missing reason, known artifact/sampling limitations, completeness, freshness policy ID/version |
| Derivation | Input evidence IDs, method/version, parameter definitions, spatial/temporal matching rules, independence groups and uncertainty/support rationale |

Do not make `observed_at` mandatory for a forecast, or force an instantaneous station observation to have a nonzero interval. Interval products require the actual start and end, not only a display label. A source publication timestamp and local receipt time are different facts. Preserve unavailable historical receipt times as unknown.

For a map click, retain the requested latitude/longitude exactly as supplied after coordinate validation and record the point CRS; obtain local timezone through a verified method. Coordinate precision does not improve radar resolution. A click may request current conditions, forecast conditions or a historical event; select and display that temporal mode explicitly rather than blending them. Use the companion [point-weather contract](point-weather.md) for the point workflow when available.

The claim record must separately contain `claim_id`, claim type, hazard/variable, proposed value and units where relevant, requested place/geometry and time interval, time mode (`current`, `historical_asof`, `retrospective`, `forecast`), evidence IDs, proposed evidence wording, and deterministic spatial/time relation results with method/provenance. Do not let an LLM certify its own spatial overlap by supplying an unchecked boolean.

## Product and hazard registrations

The following are semantic distinctions, not physical range thresholds or an exhaustive product catalog. A real adapter must register supported units, sentinels, quality flags, domain, intervals and cadence from verified product documentation.

| Hazard or variable | Preserve value kind and units | Required scope/interpretation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Reflectivity | Equivalent reflectivity factor, dBZ; sampled field | Site/elevation/range or mosaic contributor, field time and coverage; not rainfall rate or hail diameter |
| Hail | MESH/other named algorithm estimate in mm or inches; instantaneous field versus interval maximum; human size report with precision | Algorithm/version, grid and period, thermal/sampling/quality context; a report is at its reported place/time. Neither establishes roof damage |
| Rainfall | Rate in mm/h or in/h versus accumulation in mm or inches; gauge measurement versus radar QPE/model QPF | Accumulation interval and missing intervals; resolution, gauge exposure/QC or estimator bias. Rate × time requires an explicitly justified integration method; one instantaneous rate is not an observed duration-total |
| Wind | Radial Doppler velocity, motion vector, sustained/mean wind or gust; m/s, knots, km/h or mph | Site/height, averaging or gust definition, direction convention, sampling time and exposure; radial velocity is not surface gust, and surface station gust is not roof gust |
| Tornado | Candidate radar signature, observed/reported event, official warning, or defined algorithm probability; no invented diameter/intensity value | Distinguish sampled circulation/debris aloft from current surface location and damage. Warning status is authoritative alert evidence, not proof the event occurred everywhere in its area |
| Lightning | Source-defined flash/stroke/event detection and location/time uncertainty, or defined probability | Network coverage/detection characteristics and event type. Radar reflectivity cannot establish absence of lightning; no detections is not a universal all-clear |
| Flooding | Gauge stage with datum, discharge with units, observed flooding report, precipitation estimate, or defined forecast/alert | Gauge/location/time and stage datum; rain amount alone does not establish inundation depth at a building. Preserve basin/urban drainage context and missing coverage |
| Snow, freezing rain and ice | Snow depth, new snowfall, snow-water equivalent, liquid equivalent, ice accretion, or precipitation-type category; correct length units | Distinct quantities, observation interval, surface versus aloft, sensor/method; do not convert liquid to snow or ice with an undocumented universal ratio |
| Temperature and work conditions | Air/surface temperature with C/F and height/exposure, dew point, RH in %, heat index/wind chill or other named derived index | Method applicability and period; forecast air temperature is not measured roofing-substrate temperature or proof of cure/dryness/work safety |
| Tropical/system-scale wind | Official track/cone, hazard probability, or wind field with stated averaging/threshold/radii | Exact official product semantics; a track cone is not a wind-impact footprint and exclusion from a cone is not absence of hazard |
| Official alerts, all hazards | Status/category and source text, event ID/revision, issue/effective/onset/expiry as supplied | Match supported polygon/point/county/zone scope including null geometry; independently preserve updates/cancellations and warning instructions. A failed retrieval is unknown current status |

Domain coverage must be checked for the actual product, not inferred from a provider being “national.” Unsupported locations, terrain gaps and unavailable local tiles remain unavailable even if the mosaic/header is fresh. Scope expansion to additional hazards does not establish validated expertise in them.

## Deterministic eligibility checks

Supply `now_utc` and decision cutoff explicitly for reproducibility. Use a small verified, versioned product-policy registry maintained separately from untrusted provider payloads; unknown products/units/policies produce `review_required`, not guessed compatibility. A provider or language model must not create its own permissive freshness/QC policy. A freshness threshold or permitted clock skew must be configured and justified, not invented globally. The reference checker accepts policies inside synthetic fixtures for repeatability only; it does not authenticate policy provenance.

1. **Structure and numeric validity.** Reject non-finite numbers, JSON NaN/Infinity extensions, wrong types (including booleans in numeric fields), invalid timestamps, invalid latitudes/longitudes/CRS and reversed intervals. Distinguish null/empty/no-return/trace from numeric zero. Decode source-specific missing sentinels before unit conversion; do not impose a global rule that every negative radar value is missing. Require product-appropriate metadata.
2. **Units and semantics.** Only apply registered dimensional conversions. Rate, amount, interval maximum, mean, gust and probability remain distinct. Validate probability range in its declared scale and preserve threshold/space/time definitions. Do not convert probability of weather into damage probability or average independent-looking but incompatible quantities.
3. **Time availability.** For operational or historical-as-of claims, evidence must have been available by cutoff. A valid time before cutoff alone is insufficient. Publication after cutoff blocks use as an earlier input; unknown availability requires review or an explicitly retrospective task. Future issue/receipt timestamps relative to `now_utc`, or future observations beyond configured clock tolerance, indicate invalid/suspect clocks. Forecast valid times may legitimately be future; this does not excuse a future issuance timestamp. Flag publication/receipt ordering contradictions for investigation without silently repairing timestamps.
4. **Freshness and periods.** Evaluate each product's local observation age and relevant interval against its policy, separately from transmission/processing age. A forecast uses run/issuance and lead-time/applicability policy, not observation-age logic. Historical observations are not stale merely because the analysis occurs years later. Historical data freshness means suitability to the requested event/as-of state. An interval maximum cannot support an instantaneous occurrence claim. Do not silently stretch a point observation across the full requested period.
5. **Locality and coverage.** Require a computed source-to-claim relation with valid geometry/CRS, supported domain and local quality mask. Use a documented deterministic boundary rule and preserve positional uncertainty in both the query/geocoder and the source. Boundary inclusion is a geometry result, not physical certainty. Nearby, inside a warning area, inside a grid cell, sampled overhead and measured at the job are distinct relationships. Explicitly unusable QC or absent local coverage blocks an asserted observed at-location condition; suspect/unknown quality requires review. Unknown relation blocks an asserted at-location condition. Legitimate contextual wording may retain “nearby,” “in this grid cell” or “within this warning area,” with source resolution and uncertainty. Even exact coordinate overlap does not prove representative roof-level sampling.
6. **Evidence kind and claim compatibility.** Block simulation as real evidence; radar estimate as measured surface hail; radial velocity as surface gust; hail exposure as roof damage; missing/no-report as a negative observation; warning as verified hazard occurrence; and forecast as observation. A signature can support a scoped interpretation, not an unregistered categorical conclusion. Cross-field co-location requires compatible sampling times/heights and a documented method; a shared display timestamp is insufficient.
7. **Lineage and conflicts.** Deduplicate copies/revisions and group shared upstream evidence. Preserve contradictory valid observations rather than selecting the strongest hazard or majority vote automatically. Record unresolved disagreement and interpretation limits.
8. **Warnings and output.** Keep official alerts on their independent delivery path. A checker rejecting radar evidence must not suppress a valid official warning. Preserve the latest known alert and its stated validity during outages while disclosing inability to verify updates; do not indefinitely extend an expired warning or infer all-clear. Final output must preserve kind, local scope, valid time, source, gaps and conditional language.

Suggested result shape: `{status: eligible_for_scoped_wording | review_required | blocked, reasons: [...], allowed_scope: {...}, evidence_ids: [...]}`. Eligibility is not a truth or safety certificate. For a blocked real-time claim, the source may still be usable for a separately scoped historical or contextual statement after reevaluation.

## Support is not calibrated confidence

Record what supports each claim: direct measurement at a stated site, report, algorithm estimate, temporal/spatial agreement and relevant limitations. These are descriptions of support, not interchangeable confidence scores. More evidence records do not necessarily mean more independent evidence. Do not compute an arbitrary point score and label it percent confidence. Numerical probability/confidence requires an explicitly defined event or parameter, method and appropriate empirical validation; a forecast probability is not automatically the interpreter's probability of being correct.

## Manageable checker and adversarial fixtures

Implement a standalone standard-library checker outside application code, consuming a small JSON fixture envelope with `now_utc`, `cutoff_utc`, `claim`, `evidence` and supplied product policies. Begin with schema, finite numbers, unit/value-kind compatibility, timestamps, as-of availability, period matching and forbidden semantic conversions. Use an explicit compatibility table and stable reason codes. For real geometry use an appropriate established geospatial implementation; until then return review for arbitrary polygon operations. Known synthetic relation fixtures test policy handling, not a real spatial engine. Do not implement weather prediction, radar image interpretation or provider ingestion in this checker.

Use table-driven expected statuses/reasons and include positive controls so a checker rejecting everything cannot appear successful:

- Valid current station gust supports a scoped station-gust statement; changing only claim scope to a particular roof blocks the unsupported extrapolation.
- A future-valid forecast with past issuance is eligible for forecast wording; the same record labeled observation, or with future issuance, is rejected/reviewed under the declared policy.
- Null, NaN, Infinity, boolean numeric value and documented sentinel stay distinct from a genuine zero; negative valid Doppler velocity remains valid.
- Same rain number in mm/h versus mm with a defined interval cannot satisfy the same accumulation claim; a legitimate unit conversion preserves quantity and interval.
- Fresh mosaic receipt but stale local tile prevents a current local claim; a correctly time-matched historical claim remains eligible.
- New official alert revision arriving after historical cutoff is excluded from earlier-input replay; it remains outcome/later-state evidence.
- A 60-minute MESH maximum cannot establish hail now or roof damage; a scoped description of that estimated grid maximum can pass.
- A neighboring hail report with rounded time/location cannot become exact parcel truth by enlarging a matching radius after seeing the outcome.
- Same storm through midnight and repeated DST hour resolves by UTC instants; ambiguous naive local timestamps require review.
- Motion anchored at 14:00 plus 20 minutes remains 14:20 when fetched at 14:12; fetched at 14:25, the conditional closest approach is five minutes past, not a new 20-minute forecast.
- Official warning with null polygon follows documented county/zone applicability; missing matching implementation yields unknown applicability, not no warning.
- No lightning reports/no hail reports is unknown without verified observation coverage; clear reflectivity is not a lightning all-clear.
- One measurement repeated by three vendors keeps one upstream independence group. Numerical report count must not become a confidence percentage.
- A trustworthy-looking estimate with explicit unusable QC cannot pass by having complete timestamps and finite values.

Test the checker against expected outputs and retain the test log, policy version and fixture hash. These synthetic checks establish only that specified contract rules behaved as expected. The real-event, qualified-review and prospective evaluation gates remain in [evaluation-protocol.md](evaluation-protocol.md); no unit-test pass establishes meteorological accuracy or public-broadcast readiness.

## Implemented reference profile and trust boundary

`scripts/check_reading.py` is a fixture-oriented, no-transform profile of this larger contract. Every claim must supply a value that exactly matches its cited evidence in matching units and value kind; missing or different values are blocked. Requested transformations require review and are not calculated. The claim interval must exactly match the source interval: the checker does not interpolate or extend readings. A current reading can describe the latest past instantaneous observation within its registered freshness limit, with its original timestamp; it does not silently turn that observation into a measurement at the present instant. Current official-alert status instead requires the current instant inside the supplied effective interval, excluding expiry. This does not check the independent alert service for newer cancellations.

Forecast evidence stays forecast evidence in historical-as-of and retrospective modes. Historical-as-of freshness is evaluated against the decision cutoff, while retrospective mode does not apply today's age to an old forecast. Forecast lead bounds are explicitly registered, measured from issuance to the valid interval, and required even during retrospective checks. As-of availability here means documented source publication by cutoff, not proof a particular historical application had received it. Replay of a specific application's behavior additionally needs its historical receipt ledger. Observation times and observation interval ends after publication/receipt are rejected rather than repaired.

The function accepts a separately supplied `registry` argument; source/product/version bindings and permitted variable, kind, units, freshness and forecast lead are checked against it. The embedded `policies` envelope accepted by the CLI is exclusively a synthetic test convenience, not an authenticated trust source. In integration, load the reviewed registry from controlled configuration independently of evidence, and authenticate provider/adaptor provenance; never let an LLM or arbitrary request choose permissive policy values. Likewise the `trusted_adapter` relation marker and target coordinates are assertions exercised by fixtures, not proof of trusted origin or geometric computation. The checker cannot detect a compromised upstream that fabricates otherwise consistent metadata.
