## How to read this file

- **Citations.** Each citation names a file in [validation-evidence](validation-evidence/) by a short key: `mrms` (W1, MRMS MESH), `pr` (W2, point reports), `warn` (W3, warnings), `swdi` (W4, SWDI), `wind` (W5, wind), `mh` (W6, Montana addresses), `geo` (W7, geocoding), `com` (W8, commercial and tools), `hs` (W9, hosting and architecture), `ew` (W10, evaluation and wording). `h1` is the H1 prototype record, which is not one of the ten files. Its refuter's record is [h1-mesh-store-review.json](validation-evidence/h1-mesh-store-review.json), cited as `h1 review`: `R1` to `R6` are refuted claims, `C1` to `C8` corrections, `rc1` to `rc15` reviewer checks, and `confirmed[N]` and `unverified[N]` list positions, counted from 0. `fN` is `facts[N]` in that file; `open_questions N` and the other list positions also count from 0.
- **Verified and refuted.** The refuter's review sets each fact's `verified` flag. A fact marked `verified: false` appears only as a correction, with the refuter's note. `h1` has one refuter pass (2026-10-18). It re-decoded every stored day and found the store equal to the raw files on every state cell, and it corrected eight statements, listed in section 1, item 7. A number the refuter did not re-derive stays a researcher measurement.
- **Window.** The evidence counts 1,826 files stamped 12:00 UTC from 2021-10-17 to 2026-10-16 (1,825 present). A file stamped D holds SPC report day D-1, so those files hold SPC days 2021-10-16 to 2026-10-15. Section 4.1 sets the build rule: the 1,826 SPC report days that end at the build date.
- **Units.** Sizes are decimal (1 GB = 10^9 bytes). MESH is in millimetres, verified from NOAA's GRIB2 user table on 2026-10-18 (OW-H5, section 9; the archive files do not state their MRMS version, and the table versions agree), so sizes may be shown in inches to a tenth, with the mm value kept. The cap in OW-H5 still holds values above 4.00 in for review.
- **Labels.** W1 to W10 are the wave-1 workstreams, one researcher and one refuter per evidence file. H0 to H5 are the staged plan, and OW-H1 to OW-H7 are the owner decisions, both in section 9.
- **Outside the repository.** The owner's request of 2026-10-17 and the working briefs are not in the repository, so section 1 summarises the request. Local downloads named in the text are not committed.

## 1. Bottom line

1. **What was asked (2026-10-17).** The owner wants "records of hail and wind within the past 5 years for the location of every house in the state of Montana", the programs that would help, and research of all weather information for each address (wave-1 brief, lines 3 to 6). Research only: nothing ships and no site file changes.
2. **For a roofer, in two sentences.** For a Montana address, the free data can show what radar estimated near it over five years, and which hail and wind reports and official warnings were nearby, each with its source, time and units. It cannot show that a roof was damaged, and a missing report is not "no hail".
3. **What free public data can build now** (evidence window: 1,826 files stamped 12:00 UTC from 2021-10-17 to 2026-10-16, holding SPC report days 2021-10-16 to 2026-10-15; section 4.1 gives the build rule; counts are verified file counts unless marked estimate):

4. **What cannot be built yet, or not at all:**
- **A damage finding, damage probability or insurance answer** from any source here (AGENTS.md line 73, rule 5).
- **A measured hail size at an address.** A radar size is an estimate for a search area, not a measurement at the address. The units are now verified from NOAA's table (mm; the GRIB units key itself reads "unknown", [mrms-mesh-archive.json](validation-evidence/mrms-mesh-archive.json), "GRIB units attribute"), so the estimate can be shown in inches to a tenth with the mm value kept. The archive files do not state their MRMS version, and the three table versions agree on these rows. One 15 km test gives 35.7 mm at the brief's check point and 51.3 mm at the SPC report point, 12.7 km apart ([evaluation-and-wording.json](validation-evidence/evaluation-and-wording.json), "Benchland 15 km maximum depends on the centre").
- **A complete list of Montana houses.** No source is verified complete. 493,975 residence-coded points against 514,803 housing units give a state ratio of 0.96, and residence-coded county ratios run from 0.48 (Glacier) to 1.36 (Sanders) ([montana-addresses.json](validation-evidence/montana-addresses.json), "Residence-coded address points"). The column identity (H1_001N) is confirmed by census API labels, but the PL 94-171 layout document was not read, and [geocoding-and-lookup.json](validation-evidence/geocoding-and-lookup.json) still lists the layout as not confirmed (section 3).
- **Wind at an address.** NCEI High Wind events carry no coordinates (2,968 for 2020 to 2026; [wind-history.json](validation-evidence/wind-history.json)); hourly station peak winds were sampled for one month only (June 2025), while daily station maxima cover 2020 to 2026; HRRR gusts are model values.
- **Per-address publication before counsel's items are settled and the owner confirms them.** The owner answered the model on 2026-10-18 (section 9.1). The structure data are offered "for no other purpose"; parcel metadata quotes MCA 2-6-1017; GitHub's Actions and Pages clauses are for counsel ([montana-addresses.json](validation-evidence/montana-addresses.json); [hosting-and-architecture.json](validation-evidence/hosting-and-architecture.json)). Parcel owner names are not to be published.
- **A hit or false-alarm rate.** The predeclared evaluation (section 8) has not been run.

- Units: a NOAA training page says base units are mm and AWIPS shows inches ([ew f8]), and NOAA's GRIB2 user table gives mm for MESH and its swath products ([mrms-grib2-tables.json](validation-evidence/mrms-grib2-tables/mrms-grib2-tables.json); the archive files do not state their MRMS version, and the three table versions (12.0, 12.1 and 12.2) agree on these rows). Show a size in inches to a tenth with the mm value, and never as a measured size at the address. Inches are mm divided by 25.4 ([ew f51]).
- Codes: -1 and -3 occur in the decodes ([mrms f58]). NOAA's GRIB2 user table names -1 Missing and -3 No Coverage ([mrms-grib2-tables.json](validation-evidence/mrms-grib2-tables/mrms-grib2-tables.json); the archive files do not state their MRMS version, and the three table versions (12.0, 12.1 and 12.2) agree on these rows). E and M agree with NCEI EG and MG in 1,037 of 1,055 pairs ([pr f18]); print E, M and U as "provisional" or "as coded" ([ew qualifier_labels]). Use nx3hail_all only for counts, with the -999 rows counted apart. ### Recommendation

**Build architecture A first.** It needs no new host, it fits the rule that the map loads only site files, its data is about 40 MB against a 1 GB limit, and it answers the five-year question for every address in the index that section 3 defines. A browser lookup reads one tile: median 6,592 B, largest 310,433 B. B waits for the owner's decision on an off-site host; it adds the raw archive for re-derivation. C waits for a decision to run a service, because it changes the static-site rule. These are design reasons; no provider is ranked.

### Gaps the evidence does not settle

- **Shards between daily runs (A).** Not in the evidence. Committing shards to main each day would grow the history against the size guidance (growth not measured), and no public artifact quota was found. A rebuild from the 0.90 GB series would need its own job: about 12 to 14 minutes by the two cited estimates (computed here), not measured on runners.
- **Units and codes.** MESH units are mm and the codes are -1 Missing and -3 No Coverage, from NOAA's GRIB2 user table ([mrms-grib2-tables.json](validation-evidence/mrms-grib2-tables/mrms-grib2-tables.json); the archive files do not state their MRMS version, and the three table versions (12.0, 12.1 and 12.2) agree on these rows); the counts leave both codes out of the positive counts, and no count reads them as zero or as no hail (section 4.3). Parquet, vector tiles and PMTiles are not measured.
- **Schedule.** Four observed runs in one P0 sample. The evidence says a schedule needs several days of observations, and a daily job needs a retry and a manual trigger ([hosting-and-architecture.json](validation-evidence/hosting-and-architecture.json): *limits*).
- **Two-minute archive.** Whether it is needed at all is an owner question: about 69 GB, more than one runner's 14 GB disk, against 0.90 GB for the 12:00 series.
- **Decision number.** The wave-1 brief calls the off-site host decision "D32 pending", but the globe plan's D32 is the default-off storm-layers flag (`research/globe/README.md` line 542). The number needs confirming before section 9 cites it.
- **Largest tile.** It holds 33,855 addresses; whether tiles need splitting is open.

### 6.2 From a swath to "addresses inside the estimated footprint"
1. Read one file. Cells are 0.01 degree, about 1.11 km north to south and 0.73 km east to west at 49 N (ew f9). Codes -1 and -3 are counted as not a value, never as zero, dry or no hail (ew pre_checks). Over 721 frames of 2025-06-25 and 26, -3 was 33.68 to 77.93 percent of the full grid (mrms f58); its meaning is No Coverage in NOAA's table ([mrms-grib2-tables.json](validation-evidence/mrms-grib2-tables/mrms-grib2-tables.json); the archive files do not state their MRMS version, and the three table versions (12.0, 12.1 and 12.2) agree on these rows).
2. Check plausibility. No rule exists; maxima of 457.4 and 199.8 file units occur (mrms f27, f60), and the archive maximum is 1,437.4, on the 2022-04-07 stamp outside Montana ([h1-mesh-store-review.json](validation-evidence/h1-mesh-store-review.json) R2).
3. The units are verified (the archive files do not state their MRMS version; the table versions agree), so classes convert at 25.4 mm per inch: 0.75, 1.00, 1.50, 1.75 and 2.00 in are 19.05, 25.4, 38.1, 44.45 and 50.8 mm (ew f51).
4. Keep cells at or above the class, group touching cells (8-connected), and outline the union per swath (ew event_definitions; docs/ROADMAP.md:70-72). That union is the footprint.
5. An address is inside only if its point falls in a footprint cell. A circle is a search area, not a footprint (ew spatial_match). The centre matters: on the 1440 file, 51.3 units about the SPC point and 35.7 about 47.0 N, 109.9 W, 12.72 km apart, so the 1.75 class is missed from the second centre (ew f60; these are mm, the unit NOAA's table gives).
6. Word it with the address-count template and its required line: "... inside the estimated footprint ... It does not count damaged roofs" and "It does not show that a roof was damaged" (ew wording.templates.address_count, required_lines.not_damage). The radar_estimate template uses a search radius and needs a footprint version. No per-address result is published before section 8 and the owner decide; owner names stay out (ew privacy).

### 8.1 The predeclared evaluation (version 1)

[ew] marks v1 "frozen 2026-10-17 before any MESH grid value was decoded by this workstream" and says a change needs a new version and a stated reason (status). Four things qualify that: the W1 Benchland value was on file when the spatial rule was written (spatial_match); the review then wrote the report-coordinate centre into the frozen text with no new version or reason (review.corrections; f60); the strata cite DECISIONS D24 for the radar sites, which the correction places in the predeclaration, line 81; and the text cites D17's 5 mile (about 8 km) case, which the radius list [5, 10, 25 km] omits. The held-out day list is not final ("name one reference first", splits).

- **Report hit or miss.** A report at or above a size class is a hit if a MESH cell at or above that class lies within R km on the same SPC day. R = 15 km primary; 5, 10 and 25 km sensitivity (spatial_match; f7, f60). Reported as hits, misses and POD = hits / (hits + misses), with Wilson 95% and day-block bootstrap intervals.
- **Object false alarm.** A connected set of cells at or above a class with no report at or above it within R km. Reported per object, as an upper bound because reports are incomplete.
- **Size error and conditional reliability.** For hits, median and mean absolute error in inches and mm. For grid points, the share with a report at or above the class, by MESH bin: a lower bound on the chance of hail. Calibration and Brier score are not reported, because Montana has no complete reference (metrics).
- **Counts and exclusions.** Always n, hits, misses, objects and days; no rate for a class with fewer than 30 reports in the held-out window. A day with a documented gap in a reference is excluded for that reference, never read as no hail (metrics; exclusions).
- **IEM LSR (primary, all rows and M-only).** VALID in UTC, MAG in inches, QUALIFIER E, M or U. 2025 Montana: 307 rows; 225 at 0.75 in or more; 192 at 1.00 in or more (f32). The IEM changelog defines E, M and U as estimated, measured and unknown (pr f18); no NWS definition was read, so the NWS side stays open (ew f34 is refuted on the IEM side). 27 rows say "estimated" in REMARK (f35).
- **SPC daily raw hail (secondary).** 189 Montana rows in 2025 (f56). Daily files hold 1.00 in and up only (f55) and largely repeat the LSR reports, so they are not independent.
- **SPC annual and Storm Events (counts; completeness).** Annual 190 and Storm Events 190, with 61 at 1.75 in or more (f56). The SPC page says 0.75 in hail continues in the annual files ([pr] review.reviewer_checks), so the 0.75 class has a source the file does not name.
