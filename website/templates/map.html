Montana weather map

The map shows NOAA's radar estimate for the past hour and the HRRR model forecast after now, NWS warning outlines and the NWS forecast for each town. Arrow keys pan the map, plus and minus zoom. The List button lists every NWS alert and each town's NWS forecast as text. The radar and model pictures have no text version yet.
